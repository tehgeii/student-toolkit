# Privacy Policy — Student Toolkit

*Last updated: 16 October 2026 · Applies to Student Toolkit for Android, version 0.11.0 and later.*

**Short version:** Student Toolkit does not collect, send or sell any data. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

## What the app stores

Tasks, notes, semesters and courses, grade calculations, focus statistics and your settings (including the optional
name, major, university and semester in your profile) are saved **only in the app's private storage on your device**. The app has no
account system and no server.

## What the app sends

Nothing. The app does **not** request the Internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere by itself.
It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services.

Buttons such as *GitHub*, *Portfolio*, *Privacy policy* and *Check for updates* open a web page in your browser;
the app itself does not load or send anything.

## Backups

A backup file (`StudentToolkitBackup-….json`) is created **only when you export or share it**, and it goes only to the
place you choose (for example your Files app, Google Drive or a chat). Anyone who has that file can read its contents,
so share it carefully. Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for this app. When you set up a new phone with
Android's device-to-device transfer (cable or Wi-Fi, Android 12+), the app's data is copied directly to the new phone.

## Crash reports

If the app closes unexpectedly, a short report (app version, Android version, device model and the error's stack trace)
is saved on the phone. It contains no tasks, notes or grades. It is never sent automatically: you can view, share or
delete it in *Settings → Crash report*.

## Permissions

| Permission | Used for |
|------------|----------|
| Notifications | Deadline, Pomodoro and timer alerts |
| Run at startup | Restoring your reminders after the phone restarts |
| Alarms & reminders (optional) | Precise timer alerts on Android 12+ |

## Children

The app is made for students and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or *Settings → Apps → Student Toolkit → Storage → Clear storage*, deletes all of its data
from the phone. Backup files you exported yourself are not touched.

## Kebijakan Privasi (Bahasa Indonesia)

**Singkatnya:** Student Toolkit tidak mengumpulkan, mengirim, atau menjual data apa pun. Semua yang kamu isi tetap di HP-mu.

- **Yang disimpan:** tugas, catatan, semester & mata kuliah, perhitungan nilai, statistik fokus, dan pengaturan
  (termasuk nama, jurusan, kampus, dan semester yang opsional) — hanya di penyimpanan pribadi aplikasi di HP-mu. Tidak ada akun dan tidak ada server.
- **Yang dikirim:** tidak ada. Aplikasi tidak meminta izin Internet, tidak memakai analitik, iklan, atau pelacak.
  Tombol GitHub/Portofolio/Kebijakan privasi/Cek pembaruan hanya membuka browser.
- **Backup:** file backup dibuat hanya saat kamu mengekspor/membagikannya, dan hanya ke tempat yang kamu pilih.
  Siapa pun yang memegang file itu bisa membaca isinya. Backup cloud otomatis Android dimatikan; saat pindah HP lewat
  transfer langsung antarperangkat (kabel/Wi-Fi, Android 12+), data aplikasi ikut disalin ke HP baru.
- **Laporan crash:** jika aplikasi tertutup mendadak, laporan singkat (versi aplikasi, versi Android, model HP, dan pesan error)
  disimpan di HP, tanpa tugas/catatan/nilai. Tidak pernah dikirim otomatis; bisa dilihat, dibagikan, atau dihapus di *Pengaturan*.
- **Izin:** notifikasi (pengingat), berjalan saat HP menyala (memulihkan pengingat), alarm presisi (opsional).
- **Menghapus data:** uninstall aplikasi atau *Setelan → Aplikasi → Student Toolkit → Penyimpanan → Hapus penyimpanan*.
